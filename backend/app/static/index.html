Credisearch

Search a product once. We pull prices from multiple sources, group identical listings with ML, and tell you which offers are real deals and which look too good to be true.

1 · Match

Each title becomes a TF-IDF vector of character n-grams. Listings with high cosine similarity are clustered as one product; storage size and variant words (Pro, Max) act as hard guards.

2 · Price

Within each product group, the median is the fair price and the median absolute deviation measures spread, so one fake listing can't skew it.

3 · Judge

Every offer gets a 0–100 deal score and a label: great deal, fair, overpriced, or suspicious when it's far too cheap to trust. Accessories, spare parts and price outliers are filtered out, and prices are logged daily to build a history.
